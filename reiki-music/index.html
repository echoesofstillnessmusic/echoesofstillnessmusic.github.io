Music for Reiki and relaxation

Each session on this channel is built around one of the four primary Reiki symbols. A soft Reiki chant is layered over ambient music and singing bowls, creating a steady, unhurried space for relaxation, meditation and energy balance. Sessions run from 15 to 45 minutes, so you can choose the length that fits your day.

Use them as background for your own Reiki self-practice, for a quiet moment of grounding, or simply to slow down and breathe.

The four Reiki symbols

Cho Ku Rei
Traditionally associated with grounding, power and protection.
Sei He Ki
Traditionally associated with emotional balance, calm and letting go.
Hon Sha Ze Sho Nen
Traditionally associated with distance and time, sending intention across both.
Dai Ko Myo
Traditionally associated with spiritual connection and inner light.
