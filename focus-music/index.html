Calm sound for a calm mind

These one-hour sessions are made to sit quietly in the background while you study, work, read or write. The music stays soft and even from start to finish, with no sudden changes to pull your attention away.

Many sessions blend gentle ambient music with natural sound, such as light rain, ocean waves or a forest stream. Pick the setting that helps you settle in.

Tips for a good session

Keep the volume low enough that you barely notice it. Start a one-hour session before you begin, and use it as a gentle signal that it is time to focus. When it ends, take a short break and start another if you need it.
