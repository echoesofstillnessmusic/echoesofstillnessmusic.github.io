One frequency for each chakra

The Solfeggio frequencies are a set of ancient tones that many people use for meditation and relaxation. Each session on this channel pairs one frequency with one chakra, with a pure tone at the centre and soft ambient sound around it. Sit comfortably, close your eyes and let the sound set the pace.
