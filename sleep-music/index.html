Sleep music built on binaural beats

Binaural beats work by playing two slightly different tones, one in each ear. Your brain perceives the small difference between them as a slow, steady pulse. Our sleep sessions use slow delta and theta frequencies, designed to accompany deep relaxation and rest.

Some sessions are pure tones with no music at all, for those who prefer silence around the sound. Others add soft ambient music or nature sounds, such as a forest at night or a quiet lake.

How to use them

Binaural beats need stereo headphones to work as intended. Keep the volume low, well below what you would use for music, and start the video before you lie down. Sessions are long enough to run through the whole night with no sudden changes in sound.
